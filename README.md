# 🐘 Happy Birthday Hathi! 💖

A special interactive birthday arcade and heartfelt keepsake created with lots of love by **Teri Dodo** for **Meri Hathi (Punyashree)** celebrating 8 years of pure friendship since Class 7th!

---

### ✨ Features
- 🎵 **Hathi Ke Gaane**: Custom playlist with 5 special songs & spinning vinyl record art
- 📸 **Memories & Yaadein**: Photo gallery celebrating school days to future Doctor Sahiba
- 💌 **Dodo Ka Khat**: Heartfelt letter styled with custom cursive handwriting & keepsakes
- 🕹️ **Dr. Hathi Ka Birthday Arcade**:
  - 🎈 *Gubbaare Phodo* (Balloon Reaction game)
  - 🎴 *Hathi Ki Yaadein* (Memory Match card flip)
  - 🍓 *Cake Banao Dr. Sahiba* (Falling ingredient catcher)
  - 🔨 *Doston Ko Peeto* (Whack-a-friend grid clicker)
  - 📝 *Hathi & Dodo Friendship Trivia* (Secret birthday wish unlock)
- 📷 **Vintage Birthday Photo Booth**: Multi-shot camera with 4 aesthetic vintage frames and instant keepsake strip generator
